Economist · Bangladesh Bank

Md Rashel Hasan

Additional Director, Chief Economist's Unit, Bangladesh Bank

Research economist with more than a decade of central bank research. My work covers financial inclusion, digital payments, mobile financial services (MFS), and monetary policy analysis. I built a district-level financial inclusion index for Bangladesh and co-authored the Bank's working paper on QR-based payments. My current research tests whether MFS has substituted for cash, and how payment fees and interoperability shape adoption.
